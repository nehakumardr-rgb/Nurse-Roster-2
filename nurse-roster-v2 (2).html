Step 1 · Nurse inputs

Tap the M / E / N chips to mark a shift as unavailable (struck through). Every nurse follows: 2 nights, then the weekly off, then 4 morning/evening days. The two nights fall on the two days before the chosen off day. Tap 2× on any day to make it a double shift: M+E on a day-shift day, or E+N on a night day. The Shift type column sets all seven days at once.
